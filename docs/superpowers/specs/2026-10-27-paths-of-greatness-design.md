# Fantasy World — Paths of Greatness (sub-project 9)

**Status:** draft for review · **Asked by:** the owner, 2026-10-27 ("desire, trait and talent … giving Claude more about
the character; a character can do anything else, not only fight: everything on the path of greatness — science, war,
discovery, love …") · **Builds on:** the agency systems (deeds, relations, life), the LLM decider and inventor, and
sub-project 8 (fights)

## 1. Purpose

Every notable character should be someone: what they **desire**, what they are like (**traits**), what they are gifted at
(**talents**), and a **path** they walk toward greatness — not only the sword. Claude, deciding their choices, gets all of
it, so its decisions and the stories told of them have more to stand on.

## 2. What exists today

| Piece | Today |
|---|---|
| Desire | One ambition: renown, artifact, magic, wealth, vengeance, family (`Ambitions`), chosen from role and traits |
| Traits | Nine opposed pairs (brave/craven, kind/cruel, …) and acquired ones (scarred, renowned, …) |
| Talents | None as such; six attributes 0–20 (martial, diplomacy, stewardship, intrigue, lore, prowess) |
| Deeds | Monthly choice among: rest, hunt, delve a labyrinth, seek a lost artifact, steal, study, forge, duel/avenge |
| Love | Marriages happen by the life system's rules; friendships and rivalries by relations; no courtship as a deed |

## 3. Proposed

### 3.1 Desire (the ambition, widened)

A character has one **desire** at a time (it can change after it is fulfilled or lost). The existing six stay; new ones
open new paths:

| Desire | Path | Greatness looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Renown | War | Battles won, duels won, a host led, a title earned |
| Knowledge *(new)* | Science | Discoveries and inventions that change the world (better harvests, stronger walls, new roads, cures) |
| Discovery *(new)* | Discovery | Lands charted, ruins and wonders found, first contact with distant realms |
| Love *(new)* | Love | A courtship won against the odds, a marriage that joins houses, a love story told for years |
| Power *(new)* | Rule | A council seat, a crown, a realm founded or saved |
| Faith *(new)* | Faith | Pilgrimages, conversions, a miracle, sainthood |
| Magic | Magic | Spells learned, artifacts forged |
| Wealth | Trade | Fortunes made, routes opened, a merchant house |
| Artifact | Quest | A legendary artifact held |
| Vengeance | Vengeance | The foe brought down |
| Family | Legacy | Heirs raised, a house raised up |

### 3.2 Talents (new)

One to three **talents** per character: named gifts, seeded at birth from their attributes and a little chance, shown on
their card and given to Claude. Examples: *Blade-master* (prowess), *Strategist* (martial), *Silver-tongued* (diplomacy),
*Builder* (stewardship), *Spymaster* (intrigue), *Scholar*, *Inventor*, *Healer* (lore), *Wayfinder* (discovery),
*Charming* (love), *Pious* (faith), *Merchant's eye* (wealth). A talent makes its deeds likelier to be chosen and likelier
to succeed (it adds to the relevant strength), and can grow (a second success) or be lost (a maiming).

### 3.3 New deeds, one or more per path

Each new deed is a monthly option, weighted by desire, traits and talents, with odds from the stats (the same
"plausible outcomes" model as fights, so Claude decides within what the stats allow):

| Path | Deeds |
|---|---|
| Science | **Research** (works toward a discovery over months); **Invent** (a named invention with a lasting effect on the settlement or realm) |
| Discovery | **Explore** a region at the edge of the known (charts it, may find a ruin, a wonder, a beast or a people); **Expedition** to a far realm |
| Love | **Court** someone (a courtship over months, with rivals, gifts and refusals) → a marriage, or a heartbreak, or a feud |
| Rule | **Seek a seat** on a council; **Plot** for a crown (intrigue) |
| Faith | **Pilgrimage**; **Preach** in a region; a **miracle** (rare) |
| Trade | **Venture** (a caravan, a fleet) |

Each success is a milestone on the path (an event, renown, sometimes a byname: "the Wise", "the Wayfarer", "the
Beloved"); enough milestones make a character **great** on that path, which the chronicle and the film's spotlight favour.

### 3.4 Claude

The decider's brief (`DecisionBrief`) gains the character's desire, traits, talents, path progress and recent deeds;
the inventor names inventions, discoveries and found places. Choices stay within the legal options; outcomes within the
plausible ones (as in sub-project 8).

## 4. Milestones

- **P1 — Desires and talents:** the new desires, talents (seeded for existing and new characters), shown on the
  character card and in Claude's briefs.
- **P2 — Science and discovery:** research/invent, explore/expedition, their events, effects and narration.
- **P3 — Love, rule, faith, trade:** courtship, seats and plots, pilgrimage and preaching, ventures.
- **P4 — Greatness:** path milestones, bynames, the "great" mark used by the chronicle and the spotlight.

Each is planned, reviewed and executed as before. Order with the other work: after sub-project 8's B1 and sub-project
7's F1, before the fights' B2 (so Claude's choices have the richer brief from the start).
